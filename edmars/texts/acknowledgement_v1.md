Before you start (about one minute)

WHAT LEAVES YOUR COMPUTER
- Your research question, variable names, summary statistics, the analysis
  code, error messages and the draft paper go to the AI service you choose.
  Error messages can sometimes include a few individual data values.
- Search words from your question go to Semantic Scholar, arXiv and Crossref.
- If you turn on the automated reviewer, the finished paper goes to DeepSeek.
- The dataset file itself is never uploaded. EDM-ARS has no telemetry.

WHAT RUNS ON YOUR COMPUTER
- The AI writes Python (and sometimes R) code that runs on this computer
  with your permissions. It does NOT run in an isolated sandbox. Your API
  keys are removed from its environment.

WHAT YOU ARE RESPONSIBLE FOR
- Results are AI-generated drafts and can be wrong, including numbers and
  citations. Check everything before you share or publish it, and follow
  your venue's and institution's rules on disclosing AI use.
- Use public-use data only: never restricted-use, licensed or identifiable
  student data. Do not use the models for decisions about real students.
- You pay the AI service directly; cost figures are estimates.
- EDM-ARS is provided "as is", without warranty (MIT License).
